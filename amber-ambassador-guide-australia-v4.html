Ambassador Guide

Create. Connect. Cash-in.

Welcome to the amber family

We're so glad to have you onboard. As an amber Ambassador you'll score cool amber merch, help students find their home abroad, create content, host events, earn monetary rewards, and become part of an exclusive student community.

Whether you're here to make extra money, build your CV, meet new people, or just have fun, you're exactly at the right place.

Your mission

Help more students discover amber. Every review, referral, event, and piece of content made by you helps someone make the biggest decisions of their study-abroad journey.
